About Sonia Kumar

Biography

Sonia Kumar was elected as the first MP for the new Dudley constituency at the general election on 4 July 2024. Before entering Parliament she worked in the NHS, including at the Royal Orthopaedic Hospital in Birmingham, and she graduated from Aston University with a master's degree in business and management.

Sonia Kumar is the Labour MP for Dudley , and has been an MP continually since 4 July 2024.

Before you get in touch

  • Sonia can only take up casework for people who live in the Dudley constituency. Not sure? Find your MP.
  • Always include your full name, home address and postcode.
  • For bins, potholes or council tax, contact the council directly – it's quicker.
