Privacy, data sources & accessibility

Last reviewed 3 November 2026, 09:05

Privacy notice

Who runs this site: Rupinder Singh (independent volunteer) (contact: s.rupinder.singh@googlemail.com). This is an independent information website, not the official website of Sonia Kumar MP or the House of Commons. Independent prototype – not yet agreed with or endorsed by Sonia Kumar MP or her office.

What we collect

  • No forms, accounts or cookies. This site does not ask for, store or process your name, address, casework or any other personal details. To contact the MP, please use the official contact details; the MP's office is then responsible for your data.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Map tiles. The crime map loads only when you press “Show crime map”. It then fetches map images from OpenStreetMap, which will see your IP address. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from this site itself; we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Links to other websites (Parliament, the council, news publishers, social media) take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

People named on this site

Information about the MP comes from public parliamentary records. Police officer names come from the official data.police.uk neighbourhood team feed. Event details in the diary are published by the MP's office, which decides what is public. If you think something about you should not be here, email us and we'll review it promptly.

Your rights

You have rights under UK GDPR, including the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA:

  • Text and controls meet colour-contrast requirements, and the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface is designed for readers with low vision.
  • Everything works with a keyboard; a “skip to main content” link and visible focus outlines are provided.
  • Charts are built as tables with visible numbers, so screen readers can read the figures.
  • Images have text alternatives; text can be zoomed to 400% without loss of content; motion is reduced if you ask your device to.
  • The site works without JavaScript – the tabs simply show all their content.

Known limitations: the crime map is visual; the same information is given in the table beside it. Some linked third-party sites may not be accessible.

If you find a problem, please email s.rupinder.singh@googlemail.com.
